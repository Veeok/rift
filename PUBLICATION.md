# Publication boundary

Explicit local Rift source export only: no parent repository/history, credentials, raw logs, screenshots or reconstructed game source. Nothing is committed or published by generation.

Reader template build: 224ebc6ae8c5. M11 updated-reader live validation is pending; no M11.5 protection is implemented. Historical report statuses are preserved; current M3–M10 results have their own build and live/blocked denominators. Local export generation does not establish a new live compatibility pass.

Dated report pins, reader builds and results may differ from the current reader. Reports supplied with --history-dir are copied byte-for-byte and remain immutable historical observations, NOT evidence of current compatibility.

Private Windows paths in inherited test configurations are redacted placeholders. Runtime resolves .env from the package root. Legacy reader protocol/environment names remain intentional compatibility aliases.
